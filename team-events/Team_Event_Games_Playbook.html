Facilitator tips

How to run a great session

Pick 2–3 games

One icebreaker + one main game + one closer is plenty for 45–60 min.

Explain → Demo → Play

State the goal, show one example, then start. Keep rules under 60 seconds.

Mix energy

Pair Bingo/Charades with a calmer game like Would You Rather.

Celebrate

Cheer winners; optional small prizes (snacks, stickers, bragging rights).

Remote tweaks

Breakouts, shared docs, reactions, and a shared timer slide.

Inclusion

Avoid personal/medical topics; allow opt-outs without pressure.

Game 01 · 15–20 min

Human Bingo

Find teammates who match each square, get initials, race for Bingo.

How to play

  1. Share the Bingo card below (print or digital).
  2. Walk around / use chat to find someone who fits each square.
  3. They write initials — one person fills only ONE square per card.
  4. First complete line (row/column/diagonal) shouts BINGO!
  5. Optional: full card = Blackout Bingo for a bigger prize.

Facilitator notes

  • Best with 8+ people.
  • Verify winners by calling a few initials.
  • Remote: mark names in a shared sheet.

Game 02 · 15–25 min

Two Truths and a Lie

Each person shares three statements; the group votes on the lie.

  1. Prepare 2 true facts + 1 lie (work-safe).
  2. Read all three with a straight face.
  3. Group votes (hands / Zoom poll).
  4. Reveal: correct guessers +1; speaker +1 if most were fooled.
  5. Rotate until time is up.

Example sets (inspiration only — use your own truths)

Set A: 1) Met a celebrity in an elevator 2) Can name every European capital 3) Have skydived

Set B: 1) Speak three languages 2) Hate chocolate 3) Lived on a boat for a month

Set C: 1) Never broken a bone 2) Was on TV as a kid 3) Collect vintage stamps

Game 03 · 20–30 min

Team Trivia Sprint

Teams of 3–5. 30–45 sec per question. 3 rounds × 5 questions. No phones.

Round 1 · General

  1. Largest planet in our solar system?
  2. How many continents on Earth?
  3. Language with most native speakers?
  4. Element with symbol Au?
  5. Year the first iPhone launched?

Round 2 · Movies & Music

  1. Who directed Inception?
  2. Which band sang Bohemian Rhapsody?
  3. Coffee shop name in Friends?
  4. Disney film with Olaf?
  5. Instrument with 88 keys?

Round 3 · Science & Weird

  1. Bones in an adult human body?
  2. Fastest land animal?
  3. True/false: bananas are berries?
  4. Gas plants absorb that humans exhale?
  5. How long for sunlight to reach Earth?

Game 04 · 15–25 min

Photo Scavenger Hunt

12–15 minutes. Score: 1 pt/item, +2 creative, +3 funniest.

1. Something older than 20 years
2. All teammates wearing same color
3. Book with a weird title
4. Plant + human posing like it
5. Most chaotic desk setup
6. Pet or stuffed animal cameo
7. Recreate a famous movie poster
8. Optical illusion with household items
9. Homemade team trophy
10. Silliest synchronized pose
11. Window views from two homes
12. Spell team name with objects

Game 05 · 15–20 min

Emoji Charades

Act silently OR emoji-only in chat. 60–90 sec. Correct guess = 1 point.

Prompt bank (title = answer): The Lion King · Titanic · Finding Nemo · Spider-Man · Happy Birthday · Standup that could have been an email · Mute yourself on Zoom · Coffee break · Ship it Friday · Out of office

Game 06 · 15–20 min

Minute Challenges

Stack Attack

Stack sticky notes/cups in 60s without falling.

Emoji Story

One-sentence story in emoji only; team decodes.

Paper Plane

Build & throw for distance.

Name Chain

Desk Stretch

60-sec stretch; vote funniest form.

Blind Sketch

Draw eyes-closed; team guesses.

Game 07 · 10–15 min

Would You Rather

Pick a side; defend in 20 seconds.

  1. Always 10 min early OR exactly on time?
  2. Beach for a month OR mountain cabin?
  3. Only memes OR only voice notes?
  4. Unlimited coffee OR unlimited snacks?
  5. Horse-sized duck OR 100 duck-sized horses?
  6. Always detect lies OR always get away with lying?
  7. Meet future self OR past self (10 min)?
  8. Funniest in the room OR smartest?

Ready-made agenda

Sample 60-minute event

  1. 0–5 min — Welcome + kindness rules + prize tease
  2. 5–20 min — Human Bingo
  3. 20–40 min — Team Trivia Sprint
  4. 40–55 min — Emoji Charades or Photo Hunt
  5. 55–60 min — Awards + next-month tease

Have fun out there.

Swap in company trivia, print Bingo cards, keep prizes light. Connection over perfection. Rotate games each month.
